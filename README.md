# Golden Vratam Pages

Build a premium, mobile-first SEO landing page for Indriya titled “Varalakshmi Vratham Gold Jewellery.”

Use this page as visual and tonal inspiration only; do not copy its text, images, layout, or product names:
https://www.indriya.com/blog/varalakshmi-vratham-jewellery-ideas

Brand direction:

Luxury Indian jewellery brand with a warm, devotional, heritage-led tone.

Visual mood: rich antique gold, deep maroon, muted ivory, warm beige, subtle temple-red accents.

Use editorial product photography placeholders featuring South Indian women in silk sarees wearing temple-inspired gold jewellery. Include close-ups of Lakshmi motifs, nakkashi-style detailing, antique gold necklaces, bangles, and earrings.

Avoid discount-led language, loud sale banners, excessive gradients, and generic e-commerce styling.

Use elegant serif display typography for headings and clean, highly readable sans-serif typography for body copy.

SEO setup:

URL slug: /varalakshmi-vratham-gold-jewellery

Primary keyword: varalakshmi vratham gold jewellery

Meta title: Varalakshmi Vratham Gold Jewellery | Indriya

Meta description: Discover Varalakshmi Vratham gold jewellery by Indriya, from Lakshmi necklaces and temple bangles to antique earrings for sacred celebrations.

Ensure the primary keyword appears naturally in the title tag, H1, and within the first 100 words.

Add canonical URL: https://www.indriya.com/varalakshmi-vratham-gold-jewellery

Set meta robots to: index, follow

Recommend BreadcrumbList and Product structured data. Use Product schema only for real product cards with actual product data.

Keyword strategy:
Use the following secondary keywords naturally and sparingly in headings, product-category cards, image alt text, and body copy. Do not keyword-stuff.

temple jewellery designs

south indian bridal jewellery

antique gold necklace

antique lakshmi necklace

necklace temple jewellery

temple jewellery bangles

antique bridal earrings

lakshmi temple jewellery

Header

Minimal luxury header with Indriya logo placeholder.

Navigation: Jewellery, Gold, Temple Jewellery, Weddings, Stores.

Add a “Find a Store” button.

Large immersive image of a woman in a silk saree wearing an antique Lakshmi necklace and temple jewellery.

H1: Varalakshmi Vratham Gold Jewellery

Supporting copy: Celebrate the sacred season with temple-inspired gold jewellery that honours devotion, heritage, and the Lakshmi in your life.

Primary CTA: Explore the Collection

Secondary CTA: Find a Store Near You

Add a small text link below CTAs: View Temple Jewellery Designs

Intro copy section
Write 300–500 words total across this section and the two sections below. Make the language warm, refined, devotional, and commercial without being pushy.

Intro must include “varalakshmi vratham gold jewellery” naturally in the first paragraph.

Explain that gold jewellery is chosen for sacred celebrations as an expression of faith, prosperity, artistry, and family heritage.

Do not make religious, financial, or product claims that cannot be verified.

Collection discovery section
H2: Jewellery for Every Sacred Celebration
Create four premium category cards with image placeholders, short descriptions, and CTAs:

Antique Lakshmi Necklaces

Temple Jewellery Bangles

Antique Bridal Earrings

South Indian Bridal Jewellery Sets

Use keywords naturally in the card text. For example, refer to an “antique gold necklace” and “lakshmi temple jewellery” without repeating them unnaturally.

Craftsmanship section
H2: Heritage in Every Detail
Include a split image-and-copy layout.
Discuss temple-inspired motifs such as Lakshmi, lotus, peacock, kasu-inspired patterns, antique finishes, and intricate hand-crafted-looking detail. Reference nakkashi-style artistry only as a design inspiration unless verified product information is available.
Use “temple jewellery designs” and “necklace temple jewellery” naturally.

Footer
Include links for Jewellery, Gold, Temple Jewellery, Weddings, Store Locator, Contact, and Policies.

Accessibility and technical requirements:

Use one H1 only, followed by logical H2 and H3 hierarchy.

Add descriptive alt text to every image. Include at least these three:

“Woman wearing Varalakshmi Vratham gold jewellery with an antique Lakshmi necklace”

“Close-up of temple jewellery bangles with intricate Lakshmi-inspired detailing”

“Antique bridal earrings styled with a South Indian silk saree”

Ensure high colour contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, semantic HTML, and accessible labels.

Make the design fully responsive, with product cards stacking cleanly on mobile.

Optimize performance: use WebP/AVIF image placeholders, responsive image sizes, lazy-load below-the-fold images, and reserve image dimensions to prevent layout shift.

Keep the overall experience editorial, calm, luxurious, and conversion-focused.
